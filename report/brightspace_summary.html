Project Summary

This project implements a student-written PyTorch Deep Q-Network (DQN) that controls the Unitree G1 left elbow joint in a fixed-base MuJoCo/Gymnasium environment, learning a three-action discrete policy (decrease, hold, or increase an internal PD-controller target) to reach goal angles sampled from [−0.8, +0.8] rad. Two epsilon-decay configurations (0.995 and 0.985) were trained headlessly on CPU with a shared replay buffer, target network, and Huber-loss Bellman update; the faster-decay configuration was selected for quicker convergence and lower reward variance. The selected DQN achieved 100% success (20/20) on the required four-goal, 20-episode greedy benchmark, matching the hand-written rule-based baseline while reaching the success streak in fewer steps on average.
